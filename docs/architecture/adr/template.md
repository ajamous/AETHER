# NNNN. Short, decision-shaped title

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX | Rejected
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Authors: name <email>
- Reviewers: maintainers who reviewed

## Context

What is the problem? What forces are at play (technical, organizational,
political, regulatory)? Why does this need a decision now? Be specific
about the constraints — vague context produces vague decisions.

## Decision

The decision in one or two sentences. Plain language. No hedging.

Then the elaboration: what exactly are we doing, what are the
implications for the codebase, what changes between "before this ADR"
and "after this ADR."

## Alternatives considered

A real list of alternatives, each with its own short paragraph
explaining what it is and why it was not chosen. "We didn't think of
any" is rarely true. If alternatives genuinely don't exist, say why —
"this is what the spec requires" is a valid answer when accurate.

## Consequences

Positive, negative, and neutral consequences. What does this make
easier? What does it make harder? What will future contributors trip
over because of this choice?

## References

Links to relevant specs, prior art, blog posts, mailing list threads.
